About me

Accelerator physicist in the LHC collimation team at CERN

I work on beam losses and collimation for the LHC and the High-Luminosity LHC, combining beam tracking with Xsuite and particle–matter interactions with FLUKA. I am one of the developers of the Xcoll-FLUKA interface. I did my PhD at EPFL (LPAP) and CERN on Moira, the transition of FLUKA from Fortran to C++.

Research

Xcoll-FLUKA interface

Coupling of Xsuite tracking with FLUKA Monte Carlo for collimation studies: crystals, beam–beam collisions, fast prefiltering. xsuite/xcoll

HL-LHC collimation

Collimation performance with machine errors and during fast failures such as asynchronous beam dumps.

Muon background mitigation

Orbit bumps that reduce the muon background at SND@LHC and FASER, designed with loss-based Xsuite matching and tested with beam in 2025.

Moira and Flair

PhD work: transition of FLUKA from Fortran to C++, and development of Flair, the FLUKA graphical interface.
